## Safety review and limitations

Ambiguous keys remain flagged: exercise-induced bronchoconstriction/ICS, biologic sequencing, unsupported lupus-pernio indication, sputum versus blood eosinophils, NIV decisions lacking pH, oxygen survival claims, and incomplete LVRS eligibility. Adapted questions can teach a supported concept without reproducing a disputed source key.

Documented legacy corrections preserve IDs: early systemic steroids and oral route when feasible in nonmild asthma exacerbations; conservative escalation wording; COPD ICS withdrawal risk, AAT phenotyping versus genotyping, selected augmentation eligibility, transplant not being a definitive cure, and biologic/chronic-bronchitis qualifications. Exact changed units are listed in each canonical coverage record.
