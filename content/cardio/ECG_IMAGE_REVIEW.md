LBBB image was excluded because of a visible PIN field; a schematic AF comparison was
excluded in favor of a clinical recording. No visible patient names were found in selected
tracings. Attribution, original file URL and license link accompany each displayed image.
Share-alike applies to the licensed image; no endorsement by the creators is implied.

Teaching support was checked against AHA ALS 2025, ACC ACS 2025, ACC AF 2023, UK Kidney
Association hyperkalaemia guidance and original LITFL diagnostic pages (links at each
question). Each tested point is repeated in a new stable lesson paragraph. The original
lessons/questions/anchors remain intact. Correct-index and all-option feedback are tested
in the browser, as are image loading and source links. Automated tests establish software
behavior and structural integrity, not clinical validation. Final teacher review remains
required under `docs/content-protocol.md`; these are explicitly labeled draft authored
